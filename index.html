Monthly Expenses

Manage your accounts, log expenses, and trace every dollar from source to category.

Created by Vatsal Gandhi

How to use this dashboard

Track every dollar from your bank account to each individual expense — with live charts that update as you type.

Getting started

1

Add a bank account — open the Accounts panel, enter a name (e.g. Chase Checking) and optionally your last 4 digits. This is your funding source for all expenses.

2

Add credit cards (optional) — enter the card name, last 4 digits, and select which bank account pays it off. The Sankey will show the bank funding the card, which funds your purchases.

3

Log an expense — fill in a description, amount, category, and select which account or card you paid with. Hit Add or press Enter. All charts update instantly.

4

Delete any entry by clicking the trash icon in the expense log. All charts recalculate automatically.

The charts

Donut — category breakdown
Shows what percentage of total spending went to each category. Hover a slice for the exact dollar amount.
Bar — category totals
Horizontal bars ranked by spend, making it easy to spot your biggest categories at a glance.

The Sankey flow diagram

Bank account → Credit card → Category → Each expense

Every ribbon in the diagram represents money flowing between nodes. The wider the ribbon, the more money moved that way. Bank-direct expenses skip the credit card column entirely, showing a long ribbon straight to their category. Hover any node or ribbon for the exact dollar amount.

If you only use one bank account and no credit cards, you'll see a clean 3-column chart: bank → category → individual expenses. Add a credit card and a 4th column appears automatically.
