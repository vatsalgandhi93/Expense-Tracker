How to use this dashboard

Track every dollar from your bank account to each spending category — with live charts that update as you type, plus import & export options.

Getting started

1

Add a bank account — open the Accounts panel, enter a name (e.g. Chase Checking) and optionally your last 4 digits. This is your funding source for all expenses.

2

Add credit cards (optional) — enter the card name, last 4 digits, and select which bank account pays it off. The Sankey will show the bank funding the card, which funds your purchases.

3

Log an expense manually — fill in a description, amount, category, and select which account or card you paid with. Hit Add or press Enter. All charts update instantly.

4

Or import a statement — click Import Statement, choose Credit Card or Bank Account, pick the account, and drop in a PDF. Transactions are auto-parsed and categorised. Review the list and confirm — anything you uncheck won't be added.

5

Track your savings — enter your monthly net income in the Savings tile and see how much you're keeping vs spending. The card flips to red if you overspend.

6

Save your view as PDF — click the download button in the header anytime to export a clean PDF snapshot of your current dashboard. Great for monthly archives or sharing.

The charts

Donut — category breakdown
Shows what percentage of total spending went to each category. Hover a slice for the exact dollar amount.
Bar — category totals
Horizontal bars ranked by spend, making it easy to spot your biggest categories at a glance.

The Sankey flow diagram

Bank account → Credit card → Category

Every ribbon in the diagram represents money flowing between stages. The wider the ribbon, the more money moved that way. Bank-direct expenses skip the credit card column entirely, showing a long ribbon straight to their category. Hover any node or ribbon for the exact dollar amount.

If you only use bank accounts with no credit cards, you'll see a 2-column flow from bank straight to category. Add a credit card and the middle column appears automatically.
